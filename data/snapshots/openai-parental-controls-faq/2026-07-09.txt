Parental Controls on ChatGPT - FAQ
Updated: 19 days ago
Parental controls in ChatGPT are designed to support families and put children’s wellbeing first. When a parent and teen link accounts, parents can manage select settings for ChatGPT and Sora, set quiet hours, and receive safety alerts in certain situations. Controls are easy to change or turn off at any time. This article explains how to get started, how invitations work, what parents can manage, what teens can do, and the limits of these controls.
​

Link your teen’s account, then choose the settings that fit your family. You can change these anytime.

Parents don’t have access to their teen’s conversations, except in rare cases where our system and trained reviewers detect possible signs of serious safety risk, parents may be notified — but only with the information needed to support their teen’s safety.

Safety notifications
When parental controls are turned on, ChatGPT will send a safety notification to a parent or guardian in certain limited situations. These notifications are designed to alert parents if our systems and trained reviewers detect activity that may indicate certain serious safety concerns involving self-harm.
Parents are notified that a possible concern has been detected and receive resources — provided in partnership with mental health experts.
No system is perfect, and these notifications are not a substitute for professional care or emergency services. They are one way we aim to help families stay informed and support their teens when something serious may be happening.
What you’ll receive
An alert that a serious safety risk related to self-harm was detected .
​
Links to support resources and other relevant information.
How it works
Our systems detect ChatGPT prompts that may relate to a serious safety concern involving  self-harm.
​
A small team of specially trained people reviews to determine whether there are signs of acute distress.
​
The parent account gets an alert notifying them of what we detected, resources available to them, and how to contact us as needed.
​
Parents can select to receive alerts via SMS, email, or push notification.
Managing safety notifications
These notifications are on by default when a parent and child agree to link accounts. Parents can choose how they’d like to be notified (email, SMS, or push notification).
Information and resources
​
We’ve gathered additional resources to help parents who may receive these notifications. We provide resources that may be helpful to parents, including
crisis helplines

Settings and Data
Where to manage:
Go to
Settings
and select
Parental controls
​
Under Family members, select your teen
​
Scroll down to find the ChatGPT and Sora settings
These settings are on and off switches for features in your teen’s account. Turning a feature
Off
hides or blocks it for your teen; turning it
On
enables it with our safety systems. Your teen won’t be able to adjust these settings choices while they’re linked to your account. These controls do
not
give you access to your child’s conversations with ChatGPT and do not provide real-time monitoring.
Please note that updates to these settings may not take effect right away.
ChatGPT
Model training
On:
Your teen’s conversations (transcripts, files, and model outputs) can help improve ChatGPT and Sora models.
Off:
Your teen’s conversations are excluded from training. This doesn’t affect their ability to use ChatGPT or Sora.
Memory
On:
ChatGPT can remember helpful details (like a class or project) to personalize replies.
Off:
ChatGPT won’t store new memories; and any previously stored memories will be deleted within 30 days.
Voice mode
On:
your teen can use voice mode in ChatGPT.
Off:
Voice mode in ChatGPT is disabled.
Image generation
On:
your teen can create and edit images with ChatGPT.
Off:
image creation and edit tools are disabled.
Reducing sensitive content
Once parents and teens connect their accounts, the teen account will automatically get additional content protections, including reduced graphic content, viral challenges, sexual, romantic or violent roleplay, and extreme beauty ideals, to provide enhanced age-appropriate protections.
Parents can choose to toggle off “Reduce sensitive content”.
Quiet hours
Parents can set a time window when ChatGPT cannot be used.
Only one time window can be set at a time.
Turn on “Quiet hours”, then select a start time and an end time.
Please note that the following features are only available in the Sora app.
Sora
Personalized feed
​
On
: Allow videos to be recommended based on your child's history and activity.
Off
: The feed will not use your child's history and activity.
​
Continuous feed
​
On:
Allows an uninterrupted feed of content while scrolling.
Off:
Limits the feed of content while scrolling.
​
Messaging
​
Allows your child to use direct messages.
Please note that the following features are only available in the ChatGPT Atlas app.
ChatGPT Atlas
ChatGPT Atlas⁠
is a web browser with ChatGPT built in. You can manage your teen's settings for Atlas directly in ChatGPT including:
Reference browser memories:
Allow ChatGPT to reference key details from your child’s browsing history in Atlas.
Agent mode:
Allow ChatGPT agent in Atlas to help with more complex, multi-step tasks, like research or shopping
.
Safe search:
Allow explicit links in search results when enabled. Learn more about safe search.
Important:
Changes apply to
new conversations
started after you update a setting. Existing conversations keep their prior behavior.
